# Proposal

## Why

New players only find Fairy Dust and Fertilizer through recipes. A Wandering Trader that sometimes sells them introduces the mod through a vanilla system and gives players a way in before they have Glowstone or Amethyst.

## What Changes

- The Wandering Trader can offer Fairy Dust and Fertilizer among its rare trades.

Out of scope: villager profession trades, and selling Fairy Jars (catching stays the only way to get one).

New art: none.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `trader-offers`: The trades the mod adds to the Wandering Trader.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

One trade registration class. Depends on `add-fertilizer`.
